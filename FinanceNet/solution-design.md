# Finance.Net

### The Ultimate Quantitative Finance, Trading, Risk, Portfolio, Market Intelligence & Financial AI Platform for .NET 10

> Bringing the entire modern Python Quant ecosystem into a unified native .NET platform.

## Vision

Finance.Net adalah framework keuangan open-source berbasis .NET 10 yang menggabungkan kemampuan QuantLib, PyPortfolioOpt, Pyfolio, Alphalens, Zipline, Backtrader, vectorbt, FinRL, OpenBB, FinGPT dan berbagai library finance modern ke dalam satu platform native .NET.

Fondasi:
- GravicodeScience
- HF.Net
- Visual.Net
- Rust Accelerators
